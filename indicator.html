The 4C Model

Four confirmation stages.

01

Context

Establishes market structure and macro positioning — where price sits relative to the broader trend and key institutional levels.

02

Confluence

Cross-references multiple independent technical conditions, screening out setups reliant on a single indicator.

03

Confirmation

Waits for price action to validate the thesis in real time, reducing false triggers pure pattern-matching would miss.

04

Conviction

Surfaces a scored, structured read — key levels, bias and risk context — so the final call always stays with the trader.

How It Works

Analytical architecture.

01

Price Data

Raw OHLCV data is consumed from TradingView's feed, preprocessed and structured for analysis across all instruments and timeframes.

02

Structure Detection

A rules-based engine identifies swing highs, swing lows and structural pivots — the foundation for higher-level analysis.

03

Multi-Timeframe Read

Structural data from multiple timeframes synthesizes into a unified bias, anchoring lower timeframe signals to higher timeframe context.

04

Visual Output

Results render as clearly labelled overlays with configurable alerts. The trader retains full decision-making authority.
