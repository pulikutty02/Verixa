Company Story

About Verixa

A financial technology company born from the intersection of software engineering and financial markets.

Our Mission

Bridging engineering and markets.

Verixa bridges the gap between software engineering and financial markets by building intelligent trading technologies, advanced analytics, and automation solutions that empower traders with better decision-making.

The financial technology industry has long been dominated by two extremes: sophisticated institutional tools inaccessible to independent traders, and consumer-grade products built more for engagement than genuine market analysis.

Verixa exists in the space between — professional-grade technology built with engineering rigour, designed to be usable by serious retail and professional traders.

The Story

How Verixa came to be.

The Problem

The gap we saw

In years of observing financial technology products, a clear gap emerged: tools available to retail and professional traders were either technically shallow consumer products built for engagement rather than accuracy, or institutional-grade systems priced entirely out of reach.

Independent traders were forced to rely on subjective community analysis, oversimplified indicators, or expensive subscriptions that delivered little substance.

The Approach

Engineering, applied to markets

Verixa was founded on a simple premise: apply software engineering principles — clean architecture, testable logic, structured data, continuous improvement — to the problem of market analysis.

This means building tools that are deterministic, transparent and consistent in their behaviour, not tools that adapt their narrative based on market conditions.

The Stack

Technology meets markets

By combining expertise in enterprise integrations, cloud technologies, automation, machine learning pipelines, and financial markets, Verixa builds tools that span the full technology stack.

TradingView's Pine Script environment was chosen as the primary delivery platform for its deep market penetration, institutional adoption, and the ability to deliver real-time analytics directly within the trader's existing workflow.

The Vision

Where this is going

Verixa's long-term vision is to become a recognised financial technology developer — a company known for producing tools that professionals trust because they are built with integrity, documented with transparency, and improved continuously.

From TradingView indicators to cloud-based analytics, algorithmic systems to HANIRA — Verixa is building toward a complete technology layer for the modern, data-driven trader.
