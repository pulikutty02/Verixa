The Verixa 4C Model

Four confirmation layers. One high-probability read.

Rather than reacting to a single technical condition, the 4C Model evaluates a market through four sequential confirmation stages before presenting an opportunity — engineered for disciplined execution over noise.

01

Context

Establishes market structure and macro positioning before anything else — where price sits relative to the broader trend and key institutional levels.

02

Confluence

Cross-references multiple independent technical conditions, screening out setups that rely on a single indicator or a lucky candle.

03

Confirmation

Waits for price action to validate the thesis in real time, reducing false triggers that pure pattern-matching would miss.

04

Conviction

Surfaces a scored, structured read — key levels, bias and risk context — so the final call always stays with the trader.
